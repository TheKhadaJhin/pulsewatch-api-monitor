# Code of conduct

PulseWatch welcomes constructive participation from people of every background and
experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and patient.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while still addressing harmful impact.
- Give credit and protect private or sensitive information.
- Help keep technical discussion relevant and reproducible.
- Accept correction and make space for others to contribute.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Deliberate disruption, spam, or repeated bad-faith argument.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact method
on [TheKhadaJhin's GitHub profile](https://github.com/TheKhadaJhin). Include links or
screenshots when safe. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and addressed with a
response proportionate to the behavior, including content removal or participation
restrictions when necessary.

Maintainers must apply these expectations consistently and avoid conflicts of
interest when reviewing a report.
